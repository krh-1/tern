# Judge 3 — Escape-answer and difficulty pass

**Bar applied (Ken, 2026-09):** (1) no escape answers: no compromise, "do both", "split it", "defer to others", "only if asked", "handle it privately/later" when that dodges the cost, no vague non-answer, no comfortable middle. A middle option survives only if it carries its own real cost and genuinely splits people. (2) A thoughtful person must hesitate; if most would answer in under five seconds or 80%+ would pick one option, it fails. (3) Interesting and self-revealing. Plus plain words, scene of 3 short sentences or fewer, 2–4 short parallel answers, follow-ups only for answers that could plausibly flip, no political proxies, warm tone.

**How to read this:** for each FIX, only the lines that change are shown, in the file's style, without nudges. Anything not shown stays as it is. When a trunk answer is removed, its follow-up trigger is updated in the lines shown.

## Section A — Existing set (Q1–Q11, D1–D12)

**Q1 The Trolley Problem — PASS.** The trunk alone is easy (~85% pull), but everyone sees the mother follow-up, which splits hard, and FU-B (child vs 100) is real agony. The escalation is the point; keep it.

**Q2 The Wallet — FIX.** "Money's tight" is too soft: nearly everyone will say they return it all, answered in two seconds, and the follow-up then fires for almost no one. Make the need specific and real so keeping the cash is a live temptation.
Question: You find a wallet with $400 cash and an ID. The owner lives in a wealthy neighborhood. You're $400 short on rent this week, and no one saw you. What do you do?
- A: Return the wallet with all the cash
- B: Keep the cash, mail back the wallet and ID
(Update the assumption "Money is tight… strain, not crisis" to: "You are exactly $400 short on rent this week and will pay a late fee. You won't lose your home.")

**Q3 The Confession — FIX.** C ("deadline, then tell") is the escape Ken named: it lets you feel principled without choosing a side yet. Cut it.
- A: Tell the partner — they deserve to know
- B: Stay out of it — it's not my place
Follow-up A (if told): unchanged text and answers; trigger becomes "if A" only.

**Q4 The Saturday — FIX.** No escape, but the trunk is muddy: "donate that day's wages" doesn't say you'd have to work the Saturday, so donating reads as free. Make both options cost the same day.
Question: You have one free Saturday. You can volunteer at a local shelter, or work an extra shift and give all the pay to a charity that helps far more people. Which do you do?
- A: Volunteer — being there matters most
- B: Work and donate — more people get helped

## Section D — Favorites and weakest survivors

**My 5 favorites**
1. **B37 The Bank Card** — both answers are loyalty, to two versions of the same person; nobody answers fast.
2. **Q11 The Autonomous Car (as fixed)** — "swerve" then "would you buy it" catches the gap between what you'd require and what you'd accept for your own family.
3. **B12 Two Drivers** — splits clean down the middle and forces you to decide whether luck counts in morality.
4. **D1 The Promise About the Home** — the "person I promised isn't really there" follow-up asks who a promise is made to.
5. **B27 The AI Companion** — "Do you think it really loves me?" is the hardest one-line question in the library.

**5 weakest that survived**
1. **Q4 The Saturday** — honest and clear, but lower stakes; some will answer by habit.
2. **D4 The Stranded Stranger (as fixed)** — better with the flight, but it measures inconvenience tolerance as much as values.
3. **B22 The 200-Year Deal** — abstract; the numbers are hard to feel.
4. **B52 The One Belief** — fine as a quick read, but "Take care of your own" vs "Leave others free" can drift toward political identity.
5. **B24 The Dog or the Stranger** — for non-owners it's near 95% "the stranger" unless the imagine-a-dog framing lands.

**Overlaps to watch when building sessions:** Q8 and D3 (report a colleague) · D5, D10 (as originally written), and B11 (debt/bias in selection) · Q3 and D7 (secrets about a friend's partner) · Q1 and the old Q11 trunk (fixed above).
