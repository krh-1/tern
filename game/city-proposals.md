## Wording and follow-ups

1. **[KEPT]** **D11 (The Silent Witness): I dropped the opening line.** The doc starts with "Something came up that we want to explore." That line belonged to the old adaptive design, where D11 was a clarifying question asked because of earlier answers. Here it's an ordinary stop on the map, so the line would make no sense. **Recommendation:** keep it dropped.

2. **[APPLIED 2026-10-03]** **D11 has no follow-up, though it's one of the heaviest questions.** "The whole truth" faces no pressure at all. **Recommendation:** add a follow-up after "The whole truth", such as "Your answer will very likely send your brother to prison." After "leave it out" or "refuse", it could be: "Without your answer, the jury will likely blame the other man." Have the judges check it first.

3. **[APPLIED 2026-10-03 for D6; D3, D5, D10 kept one-step]** **D3, D5, D6 and D10 have no follow-ups.** They're one-step questions in the docs. That's fine for the lighter ones (D5, D10). D6 (the pension leak) is heavy, so one answer can't show why you chose it. **Recommendation:** give D6 a follow-up for both answers. After "Go public": "Your lawyer says the lawsuit would take your savings and your home." After "Stay quiet": "One of the 3,000 is your old mentor." Judges should check it.

4. **[APPLIED 2026-10-03 for B41 and B03; APPLIED 2026-10-04 for Q10 and B34]** **Several follow-ups only put pressure on one side.** B03 (the reference) only tests people who leave out the problems. B41 (credit) only tests people who correct the boss. Q10 (the platform) skips people who confront the harasser. B34 (the pill) only tests people who'd set the person free.    - B41, after "Let it go": "Your coworker finds out and asks if you said anything."
   - B03, after "The honest picture": "The hiring manager says your answer will decide it."

6. **[KEPT]** **B12 (Two Drivers) asks for an opinion, not a choice.** It still says a lot (do you judge by the choice or by the result?), so I made it light. **Recommendation:** keep it.
