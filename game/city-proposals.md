# The City: questions for Ken to rule on

None of these changes have been applied. The question and answer wording in `game/content-city.js` is word for word from the docs: the bank's "Scene" and its bold question are joined into one prompt, as in the park and the Neighborhood, and dashes in answers became periods. Each item below ends with my recommendation.

## Wording and follow-ups

1. **D11 (The Silent Witness): I dropped the opening line.** The doc starts with "Something came up that we want to explore." That line belonged to the old adaptive design, where D11 was a clarifying question asked because of earlier answers. Here it's an ordinary stop on the map, so the line would make no sense. **Recommendation:** keep it dropped.

2. **D11 has no follow-up, though it's one of the heaviest questions.** "The whole truth" faces no pressure at all. **Recommendation:** add a follow-up after "The whole truth", such as "Your answer will very likely send your brother to prison." After "leave it out" or "refuse", it could be: "Without your answer, the jury will likely blame the other man." Have the judges check it first.

3. **D3, D5, D6 and D10 have no follow-ups.** They're one-step questions in the docs. That's fine for the lighter ones (D5, D10). D6 (the pension leak) is heavy, so one answer can't show why you chose it. **Recommendation:** give D6 a follow-up for both answers. After "Go public": "Your lawyer says the lawsuit would take your savings and your home." After "Stay quiet": "One of the 3,000 is your old mentor." Judges should check it.

4. **Several follow-ups only put pressure on one side.** B03 (the reference) only tests people who leave out the problems. B41 (credit) only tests people who correct the boss. Q10 (the platform) skips people who confront the harasser. B34 (the pill) only tests people who'd set the person free. Ken's rule is to "give follow-ups to both sides where possible". **Recommendation:** add the missing side to B41 and B03 first, since both are quick to answer and could be close to the 80% line:
   - B41, after "Let it go": "Your coworker finds out and asks if you said anything."
   - B03, after "The honest picture": "The hiring manager says your answer will decide it."

5. **B30 (The Permit Fee) never uses the word "bribe".** The question says "a small cash 'fee'", which is fair, since that's how locals see it. My note for refusing says "bribe", and my note for paying says "fee". **Recommendation:** keep it as is. The question stays neutral, and only the person who refused gets the stronger word.

6. **B12 (Two Drivers) asks for an opinion, not a choice.** It still says a lot (do you judge by the choice or by the result?), so I made it light. **Recommendation:** keep it.

## Escape answers

7. **B32, "I leave the country for good."** This could look like a way around the choice between serving and prison. But leaving your home for good is a real and lasting cost, so I think it passes the rule. I scored it as looking out for yourself (Individual), and as breaking a rule you think is wrong. **Recommendation:** keep it.

8. **Q10, "Go stand with the target."** This is a middle answer, between confronting and staying put. It carries its own cost ("even if the harasser turns on me"), and the follow-up then tests it when things turn physical. **Recommendation:** keep it.

9. **D11, "Everything but that. I leave it out."** This is a middle answer, but the doc's assumption says it's a lie by omission under oath, so it has a real cost. **Recommendation:** keep it. I gave it a small lean toward outcomes, and "refuse to testify" a small lean toward rules, so the two answers don't score the same.

10. **B41, "Let it go."** This is a passive answer. Its cost is that you keep credit that isn't yours. **Recommendation:** keep it, with the follow-up suggested in item 4.

## Scoring calls I made that differ from the old docs

The axis meanings changed after the old doc nudges were written. I re-checked every one against today's meanings.

11. **Q11 (The Autonomous Car): Loyalty taken out of the main question.** Everyone involved is a stranger, so "people close to you count more" doesn't apply. Loyalty now appears only in the follow-up that asks about your own family: "No" leans Loyalty +2 and "Yes" leans Principle 2. "The car shouldn't turn on its passenger" is now scored as Rules, not Loyalty.

12. **Q8 (The Coworker): reporting is no longer scored as Rules over outcomes.** The old doc had "Report it" at O-2. Reporting a colleague you like is mainly about holding them to the same standard as anyone (Principle), and working within the rules (System). In the follow-up, "I report it now" after learning the theft hurts a small family business leans Outcomes +1, not Rules. You change your mind because of the harm.

13. **Q9 (The Protest): Loyalty taken out of the main question.** No one close to you is involved until the follow-up. "Protect the process" is now System 3 and Thought 1. I dropped Rules, because people protect the process for both reasons.

14. **Q10 (The Bystander): System taken out.** No staff are present and no rule is involved, so the old System/Disruption nudges didn't fit. Loyalty is gone too, because the target is a stranger. Q10 now measures only Collective vs Individual and Heart vs Thought.

15. **D5 (The Unearned Advantage): Outcomes vs Rules replaced** with a light Collective (hire the stronger candidate) and Heart (hire your mentor's child).

16. **D10 (The Returning Favor): System added.** "The list is the list" means working within a system you run, so it scores System 2, and moving him up scores Disruption 2. Loyalty stays as the doc had it.

17. **Judgment calls to check:**
    - **B30:** refusing the cash "fee" leans System 1, and paying leans Disruption 1. You keep to the official rule at a cost, or you break it. This is light, because some people see paying as just going along with the system.
    - **B12:** "What actually happened has to matter in the law" leans System 1.
    - **B41:** scored only on Collective vs Individual.

## Balance

**System vs Disruption is the City's strongest axis.** It has the most total signal (46.5, against 43 for Outcomes vs Rules), and 12 of the 19 questions probe it strongly (signal of 2 or more).

**One weak spot:** Heart vs Thought (30) and Loyalty vs Principle (29.5) are the lightest axes, but each still has 8 to 9 questions probing it strongly.

## New tendencies

Five new tendencies:
- "You speak up when something's wrong at work."
- "You own up, even when no one would know."
- "You think wrongdoing should always be punished."
- "You stay loyal to people who helped you."
- "You protect your job first."

**"You protect your job first" has no share line, on purpose.** Like "You'll take a gain that costs strangers", it reads badly out of context, so it can never be sent.

**Several older tendencies now also draw on City answers.** "You follow the rules" gains the most: 10 supporting answers and 9 against. Expect it to show up often in the City chapter.
