## Escape answers and middle answers

5. **[APPLIED 2026-10-04]** **B23, "Then I'd leave, or act."** This follow-up answer joins two different things: walking away (keeping yourself out of it) and freeing the child (breaking the city's order). A player can pick it without choosing between them. **Recommendation:** split it into "Then I'd leave." and "Then I'd free them." I scored it now as Heart and Disruption, light.

6. **[APPLIED 2026-10-04]** **B15, "I go."** Sacrificing yourself costs the most, so it isn't an escape. But in a made-up scene it's cheap to claim, and it makes the player look good. **Recommendation:** keep it, and later give it a follow-up: "The others say you're the strongest rower, and the boat needs you."

7. **[APPLIED 2026-10-04]** **B25, "I already don't."** People who don't eat meat face no pressure at all, and some skip meat for health or religion, not because of the animal. I scored it as a line you won't cross (Rules 2). **Recommendation:** lower it to Rules 1, or add a follow-up for it ("Would you eat meat if it were the only food for your hungry family?").
