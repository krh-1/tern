## The quick reads (B50, B51, B52)

1. **The bank allows at most two quick reads per sitting.** The rule was written for a fixed sequence, where three one-move questions in a row would feel thin. On a free-roam map the player picks the order, and the three quick reads are three separate telescopes. Someone could still answer all three back to back. **Recommendation:** keep all three as stops and don't enforce the rule. Ask the map builder to place the three telescopes far apart, so they don't sit side by side.

2. **Quick reads count toward the unlock ladder like any other stop.** With 3 of the 18 stops answered in one tap each, a player reaches the 4-answer unlock faster. **Recommendation:** keep it. They're still real answers, and the ladder only needs 12 of 18 for the chapter.

3. **Each quick-read option leans one way only.** B50, B51 and B52 have four options, each tied to one value (for example, "You're a coward" hurting most leans toward Disruption, because it puts courage first). So each pick moves one or two compass pairs a little. **Recommendation:** keep it, and check these mappings, since they're the most interpretive in the world:
   - B50: cruel = Heart, dishonest = Rules, coward = Disruption, unfair = Principle.
   - B51: break a promise = Outcomes, steal = Disruption, cheat = light Disruption and Individual, betray a secret = Principle.
   - B52: every life counts equally = Principle, keep your word = Rules, take care of your own = Loyalty, leave others free = Individual.

4. **B52's wording needed one extra word.** In the bank, "everyone on earth truly holds it" sits between two dashes in the middle of the question. Swapping those dashes for commas alone doesn't make a sentence, so it now reads "If you could make one belief universal, so everyone on earth truly holds it, which would you pick?" **Recommendation:** approve "so".

5. **The quick reads are marked `quick: true`** in the content, in case the map or the card wants to show them differently (no scene, a telescope). Nothing reads it yet. **Recommendation:** keep it.
