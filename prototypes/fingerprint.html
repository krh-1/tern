Character Fingerprint

Tern · design exploration, round two · 6 October 2026 · nothing in the game has changed

Something of yours that appears slowly around your character as you answer, shaped by your compass. Two people never get the same one.

What changed since round one. You found Grain read as stretch marks, and warned that circles could read as measles. The deeper lesson: anything printed on the body reads as skin, so every pattern on the body risks a skin condition. This round moves the fingerprint off the body: a glow around you, a little object that floats with you, the marks you leave as you walk. I checked each idea for what it could be mistaken for, and dropped the ones that failed (listed near the end with the reasons).

RecommendationAura. A soft watercolour glow behind your character, in two colours chosen by your compass. It starts as a faint wash after your first answers, is clearly yours by the time your code appears, and grows wider and richer in each later world. Auras are something people already enjoy reading and comparing ("what's your aura?"), it's the only idea that still shows clearly at walking size on the map, and it never touches the character. It does bring colour into a black-and-white world, which is your call.

If you'd rather keep pure ink: Mobile. A tiny Calder-style mobile: cut-paper shapes hanging from fine balanced arms, floating beside your character. It gains a shape at each step. It's charming and keeps the world black and white, but it's busier and harder to see on the map.

Your calls (details at the end):

  1. Which direction: Aura (recommended), Mobile, or Trail (the marks your steps leave).
  2. Colour: Aura only works in colour. Choosing it means the fingerprint becomes the one coloured thing in the world.
  3. When it changes: only at unlock steps (recommended), never right after a single answer, so players can't steer it.

One colour per letter (round three)

Your idea: give each of the ten letters its own colour, and let your aura blend your five. Each letter gets one muted pigment. Your five sit around you as one soft, continuous blend, more like the sheen on an opal than a striped rainbow. The stronger you lean on a pair, the more room and colour its letter gets; a pair near the middle shows only faintly. A colour joins your aura when its pair appears on your compass, so the aura literally fills in as your compass does.

How it grows

Mof with an Aura, for one sample compass, at the moments it changes. It only changes at unlock steps (the same moments the portrait panel reveals something), and eases in over about two seconds.

Early on it's pale and generic; it becomes more yours as the evidence builds. Through the park it finds its two colours and its shape. Each later world adds a wider, fainter wash, so a player who has walked every world carries a fuller glow than one who has just finished the park.

Three directions

Each reads the same five value pairs, but turns them into different qualities. Six sample compasses, at park done (12 answers, when your code appears) and every world walked (all 75). The five letters are the code, shown here only to tell the samples apart.

On every character

All three directions sit outside the body, so they work the same on every character, including Sumi's all-ink body and the characters that already wear a pattern (Nib, Zig). Shown at the end of the game, one compass per column.

At map size

The real walking size on the map (no zoom), with the closest map zoom (1.8×) beside it. Aura stays obvious. Mobile becomes a small cluster of specks. Trail is faint but noticeable, mostly because it moves with you.

Try it

Move the compass and the answer count and watch it shift; changes ease in slowly, as they would in the game. "Walk" sends the character around a loop: the Trail appears behind it and the Mobile sways. "Another player" keeps the compass and answers but changes the seed, to show two people with identical answers still differ.

Tried and dropped

Every idea was checked for what it could be mistaken for. These failed:

IdeaWhat it read as
Patterns on the body (round one: Grain, Strata, Speckle, Stitch)Skin: stretch marks, measles, stubble; or clothing (a sweater, a basket)
Seal: an engraved emblem on the ground under youA doily or rug; with some compasses, a puddle

Loose floating shapes (before they hung from a mobile)Flying debris, like something had burst

Rules it keeps

  • Nothing on the skin. The character's body is never marked. (Proposed as a standing rule; see your calls.)
  • No negative reads. No dots or rings on the body, no arrows, no stitches, no cracks, no puddles, no smoke, smell clouds or smell lines. The Aura's colours leave out yellow-green (a cartoon smell cloud) and true red (anger).
  • It must not prime answers. If players can see which answer makes which change, some will answer for the look they want, and the scoring stops being honest. So it changes only at unlock steps, never right after one answer; nothing explains which pair drives which quality; every compass gets an equally good-looking result; and a hidden seed blurs any one-to-one reading.
  • Calm. The Aura never pulses. The Mobile sways very slowly. Trail marks stay where they land and fade after a few seconds; they never slide.
  • No wayfinding. The Trail only shows where you've been, never where to go.

Your calls

  1. 1 · Which direction?RecommendationAura. It's the most attachable (people already talk about "their aura"), the clearest on the map and in a shared image, and the least likely to be misread, because it never touches the character. Mobile is the pick if colour is out. Trail works best as an extra on top of either, since it only exists while you walk.
  2. 2 · Colour?RecommendationYes, for the fingerprint only. Everything else stays ink on paper, so your Aura is the one coloured thing in the world, which is exactly what makes it feel like yours. This reverses the ratified black-and-white rule, so it needs your explicit yes.
  3. 3 · When does it change?RecommendationOnly at unlock steps, easing in slowly, the same moments the portrait panel changes. Never after a single answer, and never inside a question.
  4. 4 · Where does it show?RecommendationEverywhere you appear: the map, character select, the question scenes (you're still "you" there) and the share card. It follows you across character changes, because it belongs to you, not the character.
  5. 5 · Unique per player?RecommendationYes. A hidden seed per save means two people with identical answers still differ slightly. "Answer again" keeps the seed, so it shifts but stays recognisably yours.
  6. 6 · A standing rule: nothing on the skin?RecommendationYes. Add to the character rules (DESIGN §0.6): "Never mark the character's body to show the player's results; anything on the body reads as skin."
